### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to search for available study rooms by date and time, so that I can quickly find a room that is free when I need it.
**Priority:** High 
**Assumption:** Room availability shown to students is always up to date.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book a free room for a specific time slot, so that I can guarantee a space for my study session.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** The system checks for overlapping bookings automatically before confirming a reservation.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel my existing booking, so that I can free up the room if my plans change.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Only the student who made a booking can cancel it.

### US-04
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive a confirmation after booking or cancelling, so that I have proof my request went through correctly.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Confirmations are sent through the same channel the student used to make the request.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block a room from being booked and unblock it later, so that I can take a room out of service when it is unusable and make it available again once it is fixed.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Blocking a room does not automatically cancel bookings already made for it.

### US-06
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a period, so that I can make informed decisions about how rooms are managed.
**Priority:** Low
**Assumption:** Usage data needed for review is kept for at least one academic term.
